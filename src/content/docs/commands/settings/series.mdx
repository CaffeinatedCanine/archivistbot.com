Use the **Series** page in Archivist's `/settings` command to control which
details appear in Archive of Our Own (AO3) series embeds in your Discord server.
You need the Discord **Manage Server** permission to change these settings.

## Open a series settings form

On the Series page, choose **Fields** or **Inactivity** to open that section's
form. Update the options, then select **Submit** to save your changes or
**Cancel** to close the form without submitting. Select **Reset Series to
defaults** to restore the Series settings.

## Series fields

Choose which details appear in the series embed. Uncheck a field to hide it:

- **Authors:** Who wrote the series.
- **Complete:** Whether the series is finished.
- **Works:** The number of works in the series.
- **Total Word Count:** The combined word count of all works in the series.
- **Bookmarks:** The total number of bookmarks for the series.
- **Started:** When the series was created.
- **Updated:** When the series was last updated.
- **Notes:** Add a Notes tab with the series author's notes.
- **Description:** Add a Description tab with the series description.

Long notes and descriptions are split across pages. You can set a separate
maximum length for **Notes** and **Description**, from 1 to 10,000 characters.
Text over the limit is shortened with an ellipsis (`...`). Each field defaults
to 3,000 characters.

## Inactivity

After five minutes without clicks, the series embed is considered inactive.
Choose which tab the embed returns to:

- **Default tab after 5 min inactivity:** Choose which tab the main embed shows
  after five minutes: **Stats**, **Notes**, **Description**, or **Don't reset**.

The inactivity form also includes a separate setting for the `/list` command:

- **Reset to page 1 after inactivity:** When enabled, the original `/list`
  message returns to its first page after five minutes without clicks. This
  setting is enabled by default.
