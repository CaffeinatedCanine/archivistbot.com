Use the **Chapter** page in Archivist's `/settings` command to choose what
appears in Archive of Our Own (AO3) chapter embeds in your Discord server. You
need the Discord **Manage Server** permission to change these settings.

## Open a chapter settings form

On the Chapter page, choose **Fields**, **Tags**, **Thumbnail**, or
**Inactivity** to open that section's form. Change the options, then select
**Submit** to save your changes or **Cancel** to close the form without
submitting. Select **Reset Chapter to defaults** to restore the Chapter
settings.

## Visible chapter fields

Choose which details appear in the chapter embed. Uncheck a field to hide it:

- **Words:** The chapter's word count. The work's total word count appears in
  brackets.
- **Chapters:** The published chapter count and total chapter count.
- **Rating:** The AO3 content rating, such as Mature or Explicit.
- **Published:** When the work was first posted.
- **Updated:** When the work was last updated.
- **Status:** Whether the work is complete or in progress.
- **Warnings:** The work's Archive Warnings, if any.
- **Summary:** Adds a Summary tab with the chapter's summary. Long summaries are
  split across pages.
- **Notes:** Shows the chapter's beginning and end notes. If a Summary is
  available, the notes are combined with it; otherwise, notes appear in their
  own tab.

## Chapter tags

Choose which tag groups appear in the chapter embed. Uncheck a group to hide it:

- **Fandoms:** Fandoms the work belongs to.
- **Relationships:** Tagged relationships.
- **Characters:** Tagged characters featured in the work.
- **Additional Tags:** Freeform tags added by the author.

Set a maximum length for each tag group separately: **Fandoms**,
**Relationships**, **Characters**, and **Additional Tags**. Each limit can be
from 1 to 1,024 characters. Text over the limit is shortened with an ellipsis
(`...`). The Characters and Additional Tags limit fields are not visible in the
screenshot, but are also available in the form.

## Chapter thumbnails

Choose whether a chapter embed includes a small thumbnail. Archivist uses the
chapter's first embedded image.

- **Show chapter thumbnail:** Show or hide the thumbnail.
- **Hide thumbnail on Mature works:** Do not show thumbnails for chapters in
  Mature-rated works.
- **Hide thumbnail on Explicit works:** Do not show thumbnails for chapters in
  Explicit-rated works.

## Inactivity

After five minutes without clicks, the chapter update embed is inactive. Use
**Default tab after 5 min inactivity** to choose which tab the embed returns to:
**Stats**, **Tags**, **Summary**, or **Don't reset**.
