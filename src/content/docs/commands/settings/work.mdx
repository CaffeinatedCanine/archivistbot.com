Use the **Work** page in Archivist's `/settings` command to control which
details appear in Archive of Our Own (AO3) work embeds in your Discord server.
You need the Discord **Manage Server** permission to change these settings.

## Open a work settings form

On the Work page, choose **Stats**, **Tags**, **Summary**, **Gallery**, or
**Inactivity** to open that section's form. Update the options, then select
**Submit** to save your changes or **Cancel** to close the form without
submitting. Select **Reset Work to defaults** to restore the Work settings.

## Stats

Choose which work details appear in the Stats section of the embed. Uncheck a
field to hide it:

- **Words:** Total word count.
- **Chapters:** Published chapter count and total chapter count.
- **Language:** The work's language.
- **Published:** When the work was first posted.
- **Updated:** When the work was last updated.
- **Status:** Whether the work is complete or in progress.
- **Rating:** The AO3 content rating, such as Mature or Explicit.
- **Warnings:** The work's Archive Warnings, if any.
- **Category:** The relationship category, such as F/M, M/M, or Gen.

## Tags

Choose which tag groups appear in the embed. Uncheck a group to hide it:

- **Fandoms:** Fandoms the work belongs to.
- **Relationships:** Tagged relationships.
- **Characters:** Tagged characters featured in the work.
- **Additional Tags:** Freeform tags added by the author.

Set a maximum length for **Fandoms**, **Relationships**, **Characters**, and
**Additional Tags** separately. Each limit can be from 1 to 1,024 characters.
Text over the limit is shortened with an ellipsis (`...`). The **Characters**
and **Additional Tags** length fields are also available even though they are
not visible in the tags screenshot.

## Summary

The Summary option adds a Summary tab that describes the work. Uncheck it to
hide the summary. You can set the maximum summary length from 1 to 10,000
characters. Longer summaries are shortened with an ellipsis (`...`). The default
maximum is 3,000 characters.

## Gallery

For AO3 works with images, Archivist can show the images in a separate,
paginated gallery alongside the main embed.

- **Show image gallery:** Turn the gallery on or off.
- **NSFW warning for Mature works:** Add a warning-only first page before the
  images for Mature-rated works. Users can click through to view the gallery.
- **NSFW warning for Explicit works:** Add a warning-only first page before the
  images for Explicit-rated works. Users can click through to view the gallery.

Both warning options are enabled by default.

## Inactivity

After five minutes without clicks, a work embed or gallery is considered
inactive. Choose what Archivist does after this period:

- **Reset gallery to page 1 after inactivity:** Return the image gallery to its
  first page. This option is enabled by default.
- **Default tab after 5 min inactivity:** Choose which tab the main embed shows
  after five minutes: **Stats**, **Tags**, **Summary**, or **Don't reset**.
