Archivist helps your Discord community share and explore Archive of Our Own
(AO3) works. When someone posts an AO3 link, Archivist can show work details
such as the title, authors, rating, tags, and summary in a Discord embed. You
can also use commands to check work statistics, download works, and browse
series.
